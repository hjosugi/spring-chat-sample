# Shipping

## Delivery times

Orders placed before 2 pm on a working day leave the warehouse the same day.
Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 working days, express delivery 1 working day.

## Shipping costs

Standard delivery costs 4.90 EUR and is free for orders over 50 EUR.
Express delivery costs 9.90 EUR.

## Tracking a parcel

When an order ships, the shop emails a tracking link.
The status of an order in the chat also says when it was shipped.
